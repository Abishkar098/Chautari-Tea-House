Our Heritage

Where Tradition Meets Sanctuary

Located on NGO Road in Bharatpur, Chitwan, Chautari Tea House is more than just a place to eat; it's a sanctuary where tradition meets taste. 'Chautari' refers to a resting place under a banyan tree, a timeless symbol of community in Nepalese culture.
