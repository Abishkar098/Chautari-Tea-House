Why people come back

A place to sit

Benches in the courtyard, a counter by the brick wall. Come alone or bring everyone.

Proper chai

Plain, ginger or masala. Order the matka tea and it comes in a clay pot.

Food to share

Buff and chicken momos, peri-peri fries, aalu chop and sadeko. Vegetarian dishes are marked.

A resting place, the way a chautari should be

In Nepal, a chautari is the resting place under a banyan tree, where travellers stop and neighbours talk. We built that idea into a tea house.

Come for a glass of masala chai at the brick-wall counter, or take a bench in the courtyard under the white canopy and fairy lights. Order a plate of momos, stay for another round.
